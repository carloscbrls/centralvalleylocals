## Engagement Guidelines

### Response Protocol

**Within 1 Hour:**
- Respond to all comments
- Answer direct messages
- Acknowledge shares/mentions

**Comment Response Templates:**
```
Positive feedback:
"Thank you, [Name]! 🙌 We love highlighting local gems. Have you visited them yet?"

Questions:
"Great question, [Name]! [Answer]. Let us know if you need anything else!"

Tagging business:
"Thanks for sharing, [Name]! We've let [Business] know about your shout-out. 💜"

Negative feedback:
"We hear you, [Name]. We'll pass this along to the team. Thank you for sharing your experience with us."
```

### Community Building

**Daily Actions:**
1. Like 5-10 posts from featured businesses
2. Comment on 3 community posts
3. Share 1 relevant local event
4. Respond to all story mentions

**Weekly Actions:**
1. Feature 3+ businesses (rotate by category)
2. Cross-promote with Chamber partners
3. Share user-generated content (with permission)
4. Analyze top-performing content

### Platform-Specific Tactics

**Facebook:**
- Use polls in groups to boost engagement
- Share events to community pages
- Cross-post from Instagram (but edit caption for Facebook)
- Pin important announcements

**Instagram:**
- Stories: 3-5 per day (behind-scenes, polls, questions)
- Reels: 2-3 per week (business tours, tips)
- Carousel: Educational content
- Feed: High-quality spotlight images

**LinkedIn:**
- Comment on Chamber posts
- Share relevant local economic news
- Tag business owners (with permission)
- Use professional tone, add data when possible

## Content Sourcing

### Where to Find Content

| Source | Content Type | Frequency |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| Business directory | Spotlights | Weekly rotation |
| Chamber newsletters | Chamber news | As published |
| City event calendars | Community events | Weekly review |
| Facebook Events | Local happenings | Ongoing |
| Instagram location tags | Visual content | Daily |
| User submissions | Stories, features | As received |

### Content Approval

1. Business spotlight → Verify business details, get permission if possible
2. Event content → Check event is still happening, confirm details
3. Chamber news → Link to official source
4. Tips → Cross-reference with current best practices
5. User content → Always get permission before reposting

## Crisis Protocol

### Negative Feedback
1. Acknowledge publicly, take private if needed
2. Respond with empathy, not defensiveness
3. Offer resolution when possible
4. Document for future reference

### Sensitive Content
- Pause scheduled posts during local emergencies
- Review content calendar against current events
- When in doubt, delay and reassess
