## Sequence 1: New Business Welcome

**Trigger:** New business added to directory (unclaimed listing)
**Goal:** Get business owner to claim and optimize their listing

---

### Email 1: Welcome + Verification Process

**Subject:** Your business is now on Central Valley Locals! 🎉

**Send Timing:** Immediate (0 hours)

**Body:**

```
Hi {{owner_name}},

Great news! {{business_name}} is now listed on Central Valley Locals, the go-to directory for local businesses in {{city}}.

📍 View your listing: {{listing_url}}

### What's Next?

To unlock full control of your business profile, you'll want to **claim your listing**. This gives you:

✓ Edit business hours, photos, and description
✓ Respond to customer reviews
✓ View listing analytics and visitor stats
✓ Add special offers and promotions

**Claim your listing here:** {{claim_url}}

The verification process takes about 2 minutes. You'll just need to confirm your business phone number or email.

---

**Questions?** Reply to this email or visit centralvalleylocals.com/help

Welcome to the community!

— The Central Valley Locals Team
```

### Email 2: How to Optimize Your Listing

**Subject:** 5 tips to get more customers from your listing

**Send Timing:** Day 3 (72 hours after Email 1)

**Body:**

```
Hi {{owner_name}},

Your listing is live — now let's make sure it's working for you.

Here are 5 proven ways to optimize your {{business_type}} listing:

**1. Add High-Quality Photos**
Listings with 5+ photos get 3x more views. Show your storefront, products, team, and happy customers.

**2. Update Business Hours**
Nothing frustrates customers more than showing up when you're closed. Keep hours current.

**3. Write a Clear Description**
In 2-3 sentences, explain what makes {{business_name}} special. Use keywords customers search for.

**4. Respond to Reviews**
Thank happy customers. Address concerns professionally. This builds trust with new visitors.

**5. Add a Special Offer**
"10% off first visit" or "Free consultation" gives people a reason to choose you.

---

📍 Your listing: {{listing_url}}

✏️ Edit your profile: {{claim_url}}

Optimized listings get 5x more clicks. Invest 10 minutes now for lasting results.

— The Central Valley Locals Team
```

## Implementation Notes

### Email Service Recommendations

- **Sendinblue (Brevo)** — Free tier, good automation
- **Mailchimp** — Established, integrations
- **ConvertKit** — Creator-focused, automation

### Personalization Setup

1. Store tokens in database tied to business ID
2. Use templating engine (Handlebars, Mustache, or built-in)
3. Fallback values for missing tokens (e.g., "Business Owner" if no name)

### Tracking Metrics

| Sequence | Key Metric |
|----------|------------|
| New Business Welcome | Claim rate, listing edits |
| Lead Follow-Up | Response rate, conversion |
| Chamber Outreach | Meeting booked, partnership signed |
