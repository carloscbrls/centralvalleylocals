About Faith RLR LLC

Faith RLR LLC is a mobile Matco Tools distributor owned and operated by Richard Regalado, serving automotive technicians and repair shops throughout California's Central Valley. With over 8 years in business, Faith RLR LLC has built a reputation for personalized service, competitive pricing, and deep product knowledge.
