## Mission

**Give every locally-owned business a chance to be found.**

Central Valley Locals exists to lift up the businesses that power our communities. Founded by Carlos Cabrales—IT consultant, mentor, and founder of Raíces Digitales—this directory was created with a simple belief:

> When local businesses succeed, families thrive, communities grow, and the next generation has role models to follow.

### What We Believe

- **No gatekeeping** — Every business deserves visibility, not just those with big marketing budgets
- **Community first** — 100% locally owned, no national chains or franchises
- **Real connections** — We personally verify every business before listing
- **Growing together** — We connect businesses for cross-promotion and partnerships

### Why This Matters

In the Central Valley, small businesses are the backbone of our communities. They're owned by our neighbors, employ our families, and sponsor our kids' teams. But they're often invisible online—buried by big chains with bigger budgets.

Central Valley Locals changes that. We give every local business a fighting chance to be found by the people who need them most.

### Our Roots

Founded by Carlos Cabrales III, a Central Valley native who grew up watching families build businesses from nothing. Through his work with **Raíces Digitales**, he's helped first-generation Latino youth and adults gain tech skills and mentorship.

Central Valley Locals extends that mission—empowering local businesses to grow, connect, and thrive.
